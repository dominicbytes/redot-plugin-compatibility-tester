# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence rigorously, and focus criticism on behavior, code, and reproducible results.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of affected people.
